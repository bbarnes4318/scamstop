ScamStop

Privacy

This covers scamstop.pvnvoice.com and the ScamStop shared feed. It is run by PVN LLC, 2800 N 6th Street, STE 796, Saint Augustine, FL 32084.

On this website

We don't use cookies, ads or tracking scripts. If you sign up for update notices we store your email address, the date and the IP address you signed up from. We use it only to send ScamStop version and security notices. To be removed, email support@pvnvoice.com.

The shared feed

When an install turns on the shared feed, the feed stores the install's ID, the name it gives itself, its IP address and when it last connected. Installs send the scam sources they block: call-center IP addresses, hosted-dialer tags and spoofed caller IDs, with the rule that caught them. The feed never receives audio, transcripts, or the numbers people were calling. Reports expire after 14 days without a new report.

Removal

If your phone number or IP address was listed by mistake, email support@pvnvoice.com and we will remove it and keep it from being listed again.

Calls on your own switch

Transcripts and call records created by ScamStop stay on the operator's own server. PVN LLC never receives them.
